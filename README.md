Objective:
Analyze Zomato restaurant data to gain insights, perform sentiment analysis on customer reviews, and cluster restaurants into segments.
Key Tasks:
City-wise Restaurant Analysis: Explore and visualize Zomato data across different Indian cities.
Sentiment Analysis: Analyze customer reviews to extract sentiments and draw conclusions.
Restaurant Clustering: Group restaurants into clusters based on attributes like cuisine, cost, and reviews.
Cost vs. Benefit Analysis: Perform analysis of restaurant pricing vs. customer satisfaction.
Business Impact:
Customer Insight: Helps customers find top restaurants based on sentiment and clustering.
Growth Opportunities: Provides actionable insights to Zomato for improving services and targeting growth areas.
Market Segmentation: Identifies distinct restaurant clusters to better understand market segments.
Cost Efficiency: Assists in evaluating restaurant costs against customer satisfaction for optimization.
Tools & Techniques:                                                                                                                       Python Libraries: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn
Sentiment Analysis Tools: Natural Language Processing (NLP)
Visualization: Jupyter Notebook, Matplotlib, Seaborn
Clustering Algorithms: K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering
